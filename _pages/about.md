# Mohammad Mostaan

**AI / Machine Learning Engineer | RAG, LLM Systems & AI Agents**

I am an AI/ML Engineer and a final-semester M.Sc. student in Artificial Intelligence at **Tarbiat Modares University** (GPA: **17.17/20**). My work focuses on building practical, domain-specific AI systems that combine large language models, retrieval, knowledge graphs, and reliable backend services.

I have contributed to a legal AI assistant using **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**, semantic search, and graph-based retrieval, as well as document OCR and ingestion workflows and natural-language interfaces to structured organizational and environmental data. My interests span applied NLP, intelligent agents, knowledge representation, and end-to-end AI product engineering.

I work with **Python, LangChain, LangGraph, FastAPI, Qdrant, Neo4j, SQL, and PyTorch**, along with the engineering tools needed to integrate ML systems into production-oriented applications.
