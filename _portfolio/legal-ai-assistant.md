# Legal AI Assistant — RAG, LLM Agents & Graph-Based Retrieval

Contributed to a legal question-answering assistant that grounds its responses in retrieved legal and organizational information.

## My Contributions

- Supported semantic retrieval and graph-based representations of legal knowledge using **Qdrant** and **Neo4j**.
- Implemented components connecting retrieval, prompts, agents, and LLM responses with **Python, LangChain, LangGraph, and FastAPI**.
- Helped integrate unstructured legal text with structured relationships.
